**Title:** Valorant crashes after training; "PMID Card Problem Generic" appears  
**Project / App:** Valorant  
**Module / Area:** Launcher / Gameplay / Training Mode  
**Severity:** Critical  
**Priority:** High  
**Environment:**  
- Device: Laptop MSI GL75 95D
- OS version: Windows 11 Pro  
- App version: 11.11  
- Network: Wi-Fi

**Preconditions:**  
- Riot Games launcher is installed and user is logged in.  
- Valorant game is installed and ready to launch.  

---

## ✅ Steps to Reproduce:
1. Launch the Riot Games launcher.  
2. Navigate to the Valorant window.  
3. Observe the yellow diamond with an exclamation mark under the "Play" button.  
4. Click the yellow diamond and see the message “PMID Card Problem Generic” with date and time (e.g., 11.04.2025 7:55).  
5. Launch Valorant.  
6. Play through the intro cinematic and start the training session.  
7. Reach the end of the training session.  

---

## ✅ Expected Result:
- The game should complete the intro cinematic and the training session without crashing.  
- The "PMID Card Problem" warning should not prevent the game from running.  

---

## ✅ Actual Result:
- The game crashes at the end of the training session.  
- The Riot Games launcher shows a yellow diamond with an exclamation mark and the message about "PMID Card Problem Generic," even though no payment information was added and no in-game purchases were made.  

---

## ✅ Reproducibility:
- 100% (occurs every time under the same conditions)
