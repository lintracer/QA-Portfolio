**Title:** Low contrast of numbers in Date of Birth selector  
**Project / App:** https://e-floor.com.pl  
**Module / Area:** Registration form – Date of Birth field  
**Severity:** Medium  
**Priority:** Medium  

**Environment:**  
- Device: Laptop MSI GL75 95D  
- OS version: Windows 11 Pro  
- Browser: Opera GX  
- Browser version: 125.0.5729.47  
- Network: Wi-Fi  

**Preconditions:**  
- User opens the registration form on the website  

---

## ✅ Steps to Reproduce:
1. Open https://e-floor.com.pl  
2. Open the registration form  
3. Click on the Date of Birth field  
4. Open the dropdown for selecting day/month/year  
5. Try to select any value  

---

## ✅ Expected Result:
Numbers and text in the Date of Birth selector are clearly visible and have sufficient contrast with the background, allowing the user to easily see the selected date.

---

## ✅ Actual Result:
Numbers and text in the Date of Birth selector are displayed in very light color on a light background, resulting in low contrast.  
It is difficult to see which date is selected.
